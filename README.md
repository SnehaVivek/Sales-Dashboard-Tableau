<h1>Sales Analysis and Business Intelligence Dashboard using Tableau</h1>

<h2>Project Overview</h2>
<p>
  This project focuses on analyzing sales data to evaluate business performance across products, categories, regions, cities, and time periods. The objective was to transform raw data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, visualization, dashboard development, and analytical reporting.
</p>

<h2>Tools and Technologies</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Microsoft Excel:</strong> Data cleaning and preparation</li>
  <li><strong>Tableau Desktop:</strong> Data visualization, calculated fields, dashboard development, and business reporting</li>
</ul>

<h2>Dataset Description</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Sales Data:</strong> Order details, order dates, product and customer IDs, quantity, discounts, sales amount, profit, profit margin, and geographical information.</li>
  <li><strong>Customer Data:</strong> Customer IDs, names, regions, cities, and states.</li>
  <li><strong>Product Data:</strong> Product IDs, product names, categories, subcategories, unit costs, and unit prices.</li>
</ul>
<p>The datasets were cleaned and prepared in Excel before being used for visualization and analysis in Tableau.</p>

<h2>Methodology</h2>
<ol>
  <li><strong>Data Cleaning and Preparation:</strong> Cleaned and organized the sales, customer, and product datasets in Excel to improve consistency and prepare the data for analysis.</li>
  <li><strong>Data Visualization:</strong> Created individual worksheets in Tableau to analyze sales trends, regional performance, product contributions, category distribution, and monthly quantity sold.</li>
  <li><strong>Dashboard Development:</strong> Combined the visualizations into an integrated dashboard featuring KPI cards, charts, geographic analysis, and interactive category filters.</li>
  <li><strong>Business Insights Reporting:</strong> Developed a summary of the findings to highlight key revenue drivers, regional performance differences, product concentration, and potential areas for improvement.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Visualizations and Tables Created in Tableau</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Total Sales by Region:</strong> Bar chart comparing sales across the East, West, North, and South regions.</li>
  <li><strong>Total Sales by Category:</strong> Pie chart illustrating the percentage contribution of Electronics, Furniture, and Accessories to total sales.</li>
  <li><strong>Total Sales by Products:</strong> Treemap visualizing the relative sales contribution of individual products.</li>
  <li><strong>Quantity Sold by Month:</strong> Area chart showing monthly variations in quantity sold.</li>
  <li><strong>Total Sales in Different Cities:</strong> Geographic map displaying sales distribution across cities using location data.</li>
  <li><strong>KPI Summary Cards:</strong> Cards displaying Average Order Value, Total Profit, Total Quantity Sold, and Total Sales Amount.</li>
  <li><strong>Category Filter:</strong> Interactive filter allowing users to explore dashboard results by product category.</li>
  <li><strong>Business Insights Summary:</strong> A written analytical summary presenting key findings and business recommendations based on the visualizations.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Key Findings</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Total sales amounted to approximately 16.36 million, with total profit of approximately 3.45 million across 240 orders.</li>
  <li>Electronics contributed approximately 81.27% of total sales, indicating a significant concentration of revenue in this category.</li>
  <li>The East region recorded the highest sales, while profitability varied across regions.</li>
  <li>May recorded the highest quantity sold, whereas February recorded the lowest.</li>
  <li>Business Laptop and Laptop Pro represented substantial contributors to overall product sales.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Outcome</h2>
<p>
  The project demonstrates an end-to-end data analysis workflow, from Excel-based data preparation to Tableau visualization, dashboard development, and business insights reporting. The resulting dashboard provides a consolidated view of business performance and supports data-driven evaluation of sales trends, product performance, and regional opportunities.
</p>
